# General Electric (general-electric)

General Electric Company has reorganized into separate publicly traded companies: GE Aerospace, focused on jet engines and aviation systems, and GE Vernova, focused on energy and power generation. GE HealthCare was previously spun off in 2023. This repository captures developer-facing artifacts for the GE family of companies.
